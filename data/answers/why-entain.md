**Short answer:** Give two or three reasons that connect Entain to you. The domain: Entain is a large sports betting and gaming group (brands include Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin and partypoker), and that means real-time, high-traffic, money-handling systems, exactly the backend problems a Java engineer wants to work on. The setup: Entain India in Hyderabad is the group's technology hub, so engineers there build the platforms rather than support someone else's. Then you: how your Java/Spring Boot experience fits and what you want to grow into.

## What they are checking

- **Real interest:** you know what Entain does beyond "a gaming company".
- **Fit:** a link between their engineering problems and your skills.
- **Comfort with the domain:** betting and gaming is regulated and sensitive; they want people who are comfortable working in it and take responsible gambling and compliance seriously.
- **Staying power:** you want this company, not just any job.

## Structure

1. **The engineering problem** (most persuasive for a backend role): live odds and bet placement during big sporting events mean sharp traffic spikes, low latency, correctness of money and wallets, and many integrations.
2. **The company and India hub:** a global group with well-known brands; Entain India is its tech hub, building for those products.
3. **You:** your experience and the growth you want.
4. Keep it under a minute; be specific, not flattering.

Keep company claims general and true. Before the interview, read Entain's own careers and "about" pages and pick one current point you can say honestly (a product, a technology area, something the recruiter or interviewer mentioned).

## Template

> "Three things draw me to Entain.
>
> First, the engineering problems. Sports betting and gaming at Entain's scale means **[e.g. huge traffic spikes during live events, low-latency bet placement, and money that must be exactly right]**. I've spent about **[5.5]** years building **[Java / Spring Boot services that X]**, and these are the problems I want to go deeper on, especially **[e.g. high-throughput event-driven systems / scaling databases / resilience]**.
>
> Second, Entain India is the group's technology hub in Hyderabad, so the work here builds products used by brands like **[Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin — mention the ones relevant to the team]**. I like that the team **[owns the platform end to end / works directly with global product teams — only if you've confirmed this]**.
>
> Third, **[one specific thing you found or were told, e.g. the team's tech stack, a talk, the recruiter's description of the role]**, which matches where I want to grow: **[goal, e.g. designing systems for scale and taking more technical ownership]**."

**If asked about the domain directly:**

> "I'm comfortable with it. It's a regulated industry, and I'd see things like **[compliance, responsible gambling controls, auditability]** as engineering requirements to get right, not obstacles."

## Mistakes to avoid

- Saying you enjoy betting or gaming as the main reason; keep it about the engineering.
- Inventing specifics (team size, tech stack, numbers, products) you haven't confirmed.
- Calling it only by its older name, IVY Comptech. Entain India is the current name; using it shows you're up to date.
- Generic praise ("big brand, good culture") with nothing specific.
- Making it about salary or location alone.
