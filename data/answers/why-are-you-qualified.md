**Short answer:** Match yourself to the role in three points: the core skills they asked for and how long you have used them, a result that proves you can deliver at that level, and a working trait that fits how the team operates (ownership, collaboration, learning fast). Use the job description as your checklist and give evidence for each point. Close with what you'd bring in the first months.

## What they are checking

- **Fit to the role:** have you read the job description and can you map your experience to it?
- **Evidence:** results, not adjectives.
- **Confidence without arrogance.**
- **Awareness of gaps:** if one requirement is missing, you can say how you'd close it.

## Structure

The "three proofs" structure, about one minute:

1. **Skills match:** the 2–3 most important requirements, each with your experience.
2. **Proof of impact:** one short STAR result.
3. **Way of working:** one trait with a quick example.
4. **Close:** what you'd contribute.

Before the interview, list the role's top requirements (for Entain India backend roles: typically Java, Spring Boot, microservices, SQL, messaging, cloud or CI/CD; check the actual posting) and write one line of evidence next to each.

## Template

> "I think I'm a strong fit for three reasons.
>
> First, the core stack. The role needs **[requirement 1, e.g. Java and Spring Boot microservices]** and **[requirement 2, e.g. SQL / Kafka]**. I've worked with these for about **[5.5]** years, building **[what you built]**.
>
> Second, I've delivered at this level. On **[project]**, I **[action]**, which **[measurable result, e.g. cut response time from X to Y / handled N requests a day / delivered the migration with zero downtime]**.
>
> Third, how I work: **[trait, e.g. I take ownership end to end, from design through production support]**. For example, **[one-line proof]**.
>
> **[If there's a gap:]** I haven't used **[X]** in production yet, but I've **[how you're closing it, e.g. built a small project with it]**, and I pick up new tools quickly, as I did with **[example]**.
>
> So I'd expect to be contributing to **[the team's work]** quickly."

## Mistakes to avoid

- Adjectives without evidence ("hard-working, passionate, quick learner").
- Repeating your CV line by line instead of mapping it to *their* needs.
- Claiming to match every requirement when you don't; a handled gap is more credible.
- Running long; aim for about a minute.
- Comparing yourself with other candidates ("I'm better than others because...").
