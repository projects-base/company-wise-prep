**Short answer:** Say the direction (for example, deeper technical ownership), what you want in the next one to two years, and the longer horizon in one sentence. Then connect it to this role with something specific, and show you are already moving: a skill you are building, or a responsibility you took.

The prompt also mentions situational questions ("how do you handle X?"). Answer those with a short real story, not with a theory.

## What they are checking

- **Direction:** you have thought about it.
- **Fit:** the role can actually give you that, so you will stay and grow.
- **Realism:** ambition with steps, not only titles.
- **Motivation:** you care about the work, not only promotion and pay.

## Structure

For aspirations, three horizons and a link:

1. **Now:** what you do well and enjoy.
2. **Next 1–2 years:** the scope and skills you want.
3. **Longer:** individual contributor, architect or manager. "I lean towards [X] and I am open" is a fine answer.
4. **Why here:** one or two real things about this team or product.
5. **Proof:** what you are doing about it already.

Directions that fit a backend engineer (choose what is true for you):
- Own a service end to end, including its incidents and roadmap.
- Lead designs across teams: senior, then staff.
- Go deep in an area: distributed systems, data, performance, platform, AI integration.

For "how do you handle [situation]?": **principle in one sentence → a real example → the result → what you do now**.

- *Pressure or too much work:* make the list visible, agree the order, tell people early.
- *Unclear requirements:* ask the question that changes the design, state assumptions, ship a thin slice.
- *Disagreement:* understand first, agree criteria, use evidence, then commit.

## Template

> "Today I am strongest at [area], and I enjoy [kind of work] most.
>
> In the next one to two years I want to [goal, e.g. own a service from design to production and lead larger designs]. To get there I need [experience], and I am already [step: course, project, responsibility].
>
> Longer term I see myself as [direction], because [reason].
>
> This role fits because [specific thing about the team, product or scale]. I would contribute [strength] from the start, and learn [gap]."

For a situational question:

> "My rule is [principle]. For example, when [situation], I [action], and [result].
>
> Since then I [habit]."

## Mistakes to avoid

- "I want to be a manager in two years" with no interest in this job.
- "I don't know" or "wherever the company needs me".
- A goal this role cannot offer (a frontend dream in a backend interview).
- Only money, title or location as the motivation.
- A rehearsed speech; keep it under 90 seconds and invite a question.
