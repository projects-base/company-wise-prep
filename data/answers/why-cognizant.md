**Short answer:** Tie three things together: what Cognizant does (a large IT services and consulting company that builds and modernises systems for clients across industries such as banking, insurance and healthcare), what the role offers (Java microservices plus React, full stack), and what you want next (exposure to different client domains and larger programmes, growth toward senior or lead roles). Then answer the notice-period part with a precise fact, and what flexibility exists.

## What they are checking

- Did you think about this company, or is it one of fifty applications?
- Do you understand a services model (client projects, possible client interaction, rotation between projects) and are you fine with it?
- Practical fit: notice period, location and joining date. HR often filters on this.

## Structure

1. **Company (1-2 lines):** something real you know about Cognizant's work. Check their current careers or "about" pages the night before and pick one point you can say honestly.
2. **Role fit (1-2 lines):** the Java + React full-stack track matches your skills and where you want to grow.
3. **Your growth (1 line):** what you want from a services environment: variety of domains, scale, client exposure.
4. **Notice period:** exact facts, then flexibility.

## Template

> I'm looking for a role where I can work on **[enterprise-scale Java microservices and React front ends]** across different business domains. Cognizant works with clients in **[banking / insurance / healthcare / retail — pick the ones relevant to this team]**, so I'd get exposure to **[different domains and larger programmes]** than in my current single-product setup.
>
> This role matches what I've been doing for **[~5.5]** years with **[Spring Boot, JPA, REST/microservices]**, and it lets me grow on **[the React side / client-facing design work / leading modules]**.
>
> I also liked **[one specific thing you found: a practice area, a learning programme, the team's project, something the recruiter told you]**.

**Notice period:**

> My notice period is **[N days]**. **[Choose what's true:]** I can **[buy out / get it reduced to N days]** / I've **[already resigned; last working day is DATE]** / it's fixed at **[N days]**, but I can complete handover early if my manager agrees. I could join by **[date]**.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Generic praise ("big brand", "good work culture") with nothing specific.
- Inventing facts about Cognizant's clients or programmes. Say only what you've actually read or been told.
- Making the answer about salary or job security alone.
- Being vague on notice period ("it's negotiable") without a date. HR needs a number.
- Promising an early joining date you can't deliver.
