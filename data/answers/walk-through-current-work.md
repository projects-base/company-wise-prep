**Short answer:** Give a two-minute guided tour, not a résumé reading: what the product does and who uses it, the architecture in one breath, your role, then one or two pieces of work you owned end to end with a measurable result. Close with what you're doing now. Everything you mention is fair game for follow-ups, so only name technologies you can go deep on.

## What they are checking

- Can you explain a system clearly to someone who doesn't know it?
- What did **you** own, versus what the team did?
- Depth: do you understand why the system is built the way it is, not just the ticket you closed?
- For a full-stack Java + React role: do you have real hands-on work on both sides, and how the front end talks to the back end?
- A typical day (one variant of this question): how you plan, code, review, test and release.

## Structure

1. **Context (20 s):** company domain, product, users, scale.
2. **Architecture (30 s):** services, data stores, messaging, front end, deployment.
3. **Your role (15 s):** title, team size, what you own.
4. **Highlight 1 (45 s):** a feature or improvement you drove, told as a mini STAR with a number.
5. **Highlight 2 (30 s):** something technical and hard (performance, a production incident, a migration).
6. **Now (10 s):** what you're working on this quarter, and a bridge to the role.

## Template

> I work at **[company]** as a **[title]** on **[product]**, which **[what it does]** for **[users]**, handling about **[scale: requests/day, users, records]**.
>
> The backend is **[N] Spring Boot services on Java [version]**, talking over **[REST / Kafka]**, with **[PostgreSQL / MySQL]**, deployed on **[Docker / Kubernetes / cloud]**. The front end is **[React + ...]** calling those APIs **[through a gateway]**.
>
> I'm one of **[N]** engineers. I own **[module or services]**, and I also **[code reviews / on-call / mentoring]**.
>
> One thing I'm proud of: **[problem]**. I **[what you did: designed the API, rewrote the query, added caching, built the React screen]**, and the result was **[metric: latency from X to Y ms, fewer incidents, release time cut]**.
>
> A harder one: **[incident or performance issue]**. I found the cause by **[thread dump / slow-query log / profiling]**, fixed it by **[fix]**, and we added **[test / alert / guardrail]** so it doesn't recur.
>
> Right now I'm working on **[current work]**, which is close to what this role needs in **[Java microservices + React]**.

**"How does your day look?"** variant: **[stand-up]**, then focused work on **[stories]**, **[PR reviews]**, **[syncs with QA/product]**, **[deployments or on-call]**. Mention how you use **[Jira / CI pipelines / testing with JUnit and Mockito]**.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Listing every technology on the résumé. Each one invites a deep question.
- "We did..." throughout. Say "I" for your part and be honest about the team's part.
- No numbers. Even rough ones ("about 30% faster") make it concrete.
- Going past three minutes before they ask anything.
- Naming something like Kafka or Kubernetes without being ready to explain how you used it.
- Criticising your current employer or team.
