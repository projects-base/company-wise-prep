## The goal

By **Mon 25 Jan 2027** you can walk into any SDE II / Senior Java backend loop and handle all of it:
DSA coding, LLD, HLD / system design, and deep Java and Spring Boot internals, answered at the depth
an experienced interviewer expects. Thirteen weeks, about 8 hours a week.

## The 8 rules (strict)

1. **The floor is non-negotiable.** 45 minutes every weekday, 2 hours on Saturday and Sunday. On a
   terrible day, do at least the DSA session. Zero days are not allowed.
2. **No catching up by bingeing.** Missed a day? Today simply continues from where you stopped. Never
   add more than 30 extra minutes to a day.
3. **Recall before reading.** Attempt every problem for 20 minutes and answer every concept out loud
   *before* opening the model answer. Reading first is the same as skipping.
4. **Every concept answer in 4 layers:** what it is → how it works inside → where you used it → what
   goes wrong. One layer is a junior answer; four layers is the depth TR2 wanted.
5. **Out loud, not in your head.** Interviews are spoken. Say it, or it does not count.
6. **The Sunday gate decides the next week.** Pass = 4 of 5 concepts clear and the re-solve done. Fail
   = the weakest problem and missed concepts are repeated first next week. No moving on with gaps.
7. **Every mock is scored and logged.** Five mocks with Claude (weeks 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) plus two finals
   in week 13. A score below 6/10 means the following Sunday's lesson slot redoes that topic.
8. **No new resources.** Everything is in this app: the Academy lessons, the question bank's model
   answers (each has an example and follow-up questions), the Playground. Do not wander to YouTube.

## How to use a question page

Each bank question has a model answer: **short answer → explanation → example → pitfalls and
follow-ups.** The follow-ups are the add-on questions an interviewer asks next. Treat each one as a
mini question: answer it out loud before reading its line.

## Escalation: when you fall behind

- **1–2 days behind:** keep going; Today continues from where you stopped.
- **A whole week behind:** drop that week's Saturday timed DSA and the second concept lesson, never
  the gate or the mock.
- **Two gates failed in a row:** stop new material for 2 days and redo both weeks' weakest items.

## Done means

At least 60 DSA problems solved alone, 10 LLD designs and 8 HLD case studies, 60 concept items in
4 layers, 7 mocks logged, and the last three mocks at 7/10 or better.
