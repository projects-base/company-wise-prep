## The daily routine

- **Pick one fixed weekday slot** (for example 7:00–8:00 before work, or 21:00–22:00) and protect it. Same slot,
  every weekday.
- **Sunday:** after the Masai class, the design hour, then the 15-minute review. Saturday is Masai only.
- **Masai backlog:** weeks 1–3 add one weekday evening (Wednesday) for a missed lecture or pending assignment. Clear
  it early so the weekends are just the class and that week's assignment.
- **AI Build:** a milestone lands on the first Monday or Wednesday after the lessons it needs. Build it in your own
  `triagedesk` repo, commit and tag it; that repo is the evidence behind "tell me about something you built".

## The 7 rules

1. **Same slot every weekday.** Start the first core step within a minute of sitting down.
2. **Never a zero day.** On a terrible day, do the floor: just the lesson. That keeps the streak.
3. **Missing a day is normal, not a disaster.** Saturday's catch-up slot takes the most important unfinished
   session (an AI Build milestone, then DSA, then a lesson). Anything still unfinished on Sunday night is dropped,
   not carried. Never double up on Monday, and never add more than 30 extra minutes to a day.
4. **Recall before reading.** Attempt every problem for 22 minutes and answer every question out loud *before*
   opening the model answer.
5. **Every concept answer in 4 layers:** what it is → how it works inside → where you used it → what goes wrong.
6. **Out loud, not in your head.** Interviews are spoken. Use Judge my answer (speak or Record).
7. **Every mock is scored and logged.** Four mocks with Claude (weeks 9, 10, 13 and 14) and two finals on the last
   two Sundays. Below 6/10 means that topic goes first the next week.

Stuck on a page? Use **Stuck?** (the Lifeline): nudge first, key idea next, full explanation last.

## Applications (from week 8)

- **Sun 17 Jan checkpoint:** resume updated with impact numbers, then start applying. Line up referrals in early
  January; a referral from a senior or long-tenured engineer works best.
- Aim for 30–60 target companies across product companies, fintech, healthcare platforms and GCCs, and for two
  offers at the same time. Target SDE II / Senior; do not accept a lower level.
- Only put on your resume what you can defend in depth. As each AI Build milestone lands, it becomes a project line
  you can talk about.
- After each real interview, its questions go into that Sunday's review, and any topic you could not answer goes
  first the next week.

## When you fall behind

- **A missed day:** Saturday's catch-up slot (rule 3).
- **A whole week lost** (illness, release crunch, holidays): do floors only that week, skip its Sunday design, and
  restart on the following Monday's plan.
- **Two weekly reviews with gaps in a row:** stop stretch for a week and spend that time on the gaps.
